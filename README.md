TripMate
Overview
TripMate is a conversational travel planning assistant. Send it a plain-text request — even short or with typos — and it turns it into a practical trip plan, from route choice and budget to a day-by-day itinerary. It is especially tuned for Indian routes such as Mumbai to Goa.

Features
End-to-end trip planning: destination advice, day-by-day itineraries, and activity suggestions
Transport comparisons for Indian routes (flights, Konkan Railway trains, overnight buses, self-drive)
Budget estimates in INR using realistic ranges instead of invented prices
Accommodation guidance by area and travel style
Packing lists and season advice (best months vs monsoon)
Session-aware conversation, so follow-up questions keep context
Limitations
Does not book tickets or hotels; it plans and advises only
Prices, schedules, and opening hours are estimates — verify current details before travelling
Answers are generated by the ASI:One model and may be imperfect for niche locations
